# Waterless Solar Panel Cleaning Robot

## Project Overview

The Waterless Solar Panel Cleaning Robot is a tracked mobile robot designed to clean solar panel surfaces without using water. It uses a motor-driven roller brush to remove dust and surface contaminants, providing a mechanical cleaning approach intended to reduce water consumption.

The robot was designed to move directly across solar panels using a tracked drive system. Its chassis was constructed from aluminum frames, with rubber tracks providing the drive arrangement.

This project was developed as part of a **Capstone Makerspace Project under the guidance of Professor Vijay AS**.

## Mechanical Design

The mechanical design consists of an aluminum-frame chassis supporting a four-wheel tracked drive system and a roller brush cleaning mechanism.

The robot incorporates 3D-printed wheels and motor mounts, along with rubber tracks and a purchased roller brush. The brush is driven by a motor and positioned to contact the solar panel surface during the cleaning process.

The design focuses on integrating the mobility system and brush mechanism into a compact structure suitable for movement across solar panels.

## Key Features

- Waterless cleaning using a motor-driven roller brush
- Tracked mobility for movement across solar panel surfaces
- Aluminum-frame chassis
- Rubber track drive system
- 3D-printed wheels and motor mounts
- Mechanically integrated roller brush

## Project Status

The project involved mechanical design and prototype development. The complete robot system was not fully integrated or validated, and its cleaning performance was not experimentally verified.

The work documented in this project represents the mechanical design and development stage.
